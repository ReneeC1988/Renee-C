Copperhollow AP Integration

How vendor invoices for incoming rock, binder and hauling reach the ERP without anyone keying them. First the back-end setup the ERP needs, then a replay of one overnight run: tickets pulled from a third-party ticketing provider, invoices matched, and an AP batch built through the ERP's API.

Every company, vendor, account and amount on this page is fictional. The APIs are mock servers built for this portfolio; endpoint names are illustrative and do not belong to any real product.
