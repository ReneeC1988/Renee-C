Equipment usage reconciliation · Month-end

Copperhollow Equipment Usage Rec

Equipment usage is posted with payroll: foremen put each machine and its hours on the crew timecard, and the payroll update charges the job at an hourly rate and credits equipment revenue to the company that owns the machine. Usage should be billed on the hours the machine was actually used. This page checks one month of those charges against the machines' telematics, corrects what's wrong through an EM usage batch, sends labor follow-ups to payroll, and ties everything out across both companies.

Every company, job, unit, account and amount on this page is fictional. The ERP and telematics feeds are simulated; field and form names are illustrative.
