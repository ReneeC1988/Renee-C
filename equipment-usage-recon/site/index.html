Copperhollow Equipment Usage Rec

Every hour a paver, roller or loader works on a job is charged to that job at an internal rate, and becomes equipment revenue for the company that owns the machine. This page checks one month of those charges against what the machines' telematics say they did, fixes what can be fixed, holds the judgment calls, and ties the result out across both companies.

Every company, job, unit, account and amount on this page is fictional. The ERP and telematics feeds are simulated; field and form names are illustrative.
