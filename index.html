AP automation · API integration

Scale tickets to ERP, without keying

A materials company receives about 400 third-party scale tickets and 25 vendor invoices a month. I built a pipeline that matches every invoice line to its ticket, holds exceptions, writes the ERP import file, and pushes approved invoices through an API into an AP batch that a person reviews and posts.

  • 22 of 25 invoices imported automatically; 3 held with reasons (unknown ticket, extra tons, duplicate)
  • A supplier's 7% price increase flagged for buyer approval
  • Segregation of duties: the integration cannot post, a supervisor does
  • The ERP batch ties to the approved total to the cent

Equipment · Job cost · Intercompany

Equipment usage, checked against the machines

Crews log hours in HeavyJob, and equipment usage is posted with payroll: about 480 timecard lines a month charge machines to jobs. I built a reconciliation that ties HeavyJob to payroll to usage, compares every machine, every day, to its telematics, bills actual hours used, and posts corrections through the ERP's own usage batch, with intercompany in both directions.

  • A milling machine's three missed days ($10,660) caught from engine hours and geofences
  • An approved HeavyJob timecard that never reached payroll caught before the operator went unpaid
  • Duplicates and stale rates fixed automatically; wrong-job charges moved after approval
  • Shift hours keyed as machine hours corrected to actual hours; operator labor sent to payroll for reclass
  • Intercompany due-to and due-from tie to the cent both ways; 9 of 9 tie-outs pass
